PERSONAL
Open templates/personal.html in a browser, copy its rendered signature and paste into the personal account’s signature editor. It contains Paul Armstrong and “Let’s keep in touch.” with the approved A monogram.

GEMBA
The reusable templates/gemba-business.html uses {{NAME}}, {{ROLE}} and two {{EMAIL}} placeholders. Replace those in a copy before installation, including the mailto destination. It uses the Gemba logo tile and editable text with Urbanist/Nunito Sans declarations plus Arial/Helvetica fallbacks. For Paul’s exact approved type, use the Gemba identity image at 288 x 76 px above the same live contact links and 3 px #79259B divider.

APP SETTINGS
Hotmail/Outlook.com: Settings > Accounts > Signatures.
Gmail: Settings > See all settings > General > Signature.
Mailbird Classic: Menu > Settings > Identities > edit the correct sending identity.

Choose the correct sending account, save, select appropriate new-message/reply defaults and inspect a new unsent message. Check image size and live links. Recipients may need to allow external images; this is controlled by their email app.
